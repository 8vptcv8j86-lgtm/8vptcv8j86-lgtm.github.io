What BrotherBench is

Who can join

You must be 18 or older.

Founding spots

Claiming a founding spot is free and needs no card. It holds your place while the Founding Circle forms. It is not a paid membership.

Membership, trial, and billing

Canceling and refunds

You can cancel anytime. Cancel before your trial ends and you won't be charged. After that, cancel before your next renewal date to avoid the next monthly charge. To cancel, or if something went wrong with a charge, email hello@brotherbench.com and we'll sort it out with you.

Circle rules

  • No selling or recruiting: don't pitch products, services, or causes to other members.
  • No dating pitch inside circles.
  • Show up as yourself: you can always pass on a question.
