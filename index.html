How a circle works

  1. Matched into a group of 6–8 We place you by time zone, schedule, and what you're looking for — not by status.
  2. 90 minutes, same day each week Check-in → one theme → commitments. Cameras on when you can. Pass is always allowed.
  3. Same seats, week after week No rotating strangers. Friendship needs reps, not endless mixers.
  4. Async chat between calls A private community channel for your circle — light, optional, no pressure to perform.

FAQ

Is this therapy?

No. BrotherBench is a peer community and facilitated friendship circle. We do not diagnose, treat, or provide mental-health care. If you need a licensed professional, please see one. Facilitators hold structure and safety — they are not your therapists.

What if I'm in crisis?

If you are in immediate danger, call local emergency services. In the United States, call or text 988 (Suicide & Crisis Lifeline) — 988lifeline.org. BrotherBench is not a crisis hotline and cannot replace emergency or clinical care.

Do I have to share deep feelings?

No. You can pass any round. Most men start light. Consistency beats intensity.

Is this a dating or “masculinity” program?

No dating pitch inside circles. No grievance brand. No pickup curriculum. Friendship first.

Can I cancel?

Yes. Cancel anytime. Founding pricing covers your first three paid months; then Core list price applies if you stay.

Crisis resource: If you or someone you know is struggling or in crisis, help is available. In the U.S., call or text 988. BrotherBench members are also encouraged to seek licensed care when issues go beyond peer support.
